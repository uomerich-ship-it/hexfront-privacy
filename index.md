# HexFront Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 10 May 2026

HexFront ("the App") is developed and published by Quantbit Studios.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect personal information
- We do not require sign-up or accounts
- We do not access your contacts, photos, files, or location
- We do not display advertisements
- We do not use third-party analytics or tracking
- We do not store data on external servers about you

## Data Stored On Your Device Only

- Game progress, level, XP, statistics
- Settings preferences (sound, difficulty, skin)
- Match history

This data stays on your device and can be deleted at any time via the in-app Reset Stats option or by uninstalling the app.

## Multiplayer Connections

- Bluetooth and Local WiFi modes connect directly between devices, sending only game move data
- Online multiplayer uses an anonymous public relay that forwards game moves between paired clients only — no accounts, no IDs, no persistence
- We do not collect IP addresses, device identifiers, or any personal data over multiplayer connections

## Permissions Used

- **INTERNET** — only for online multiplayer relay
- **BLUETOOTH** — only for Bluetooth multiplayer
- **WIFI** — only for local WiFi multiplayer

## Children

HexFront is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
